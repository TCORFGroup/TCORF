Breadth first: level -1 and the holdings register before anything deeper.
Mark everything not reached "not reached (time limit)" — never "not found". In the completeness check this is a 5th verdict, and it does not count toward the INCOMPLETE RUN line.

# Task
For the 1 jurisdiction I name - a country, or a state, province or city inside one - find what it is made of. Country, state and city always written out, no abbreviations.
Look first for: the entity annex or schedule of the budget; a government organisation register; the list of auditees published by the audit institution; the consolidated reporting entity list in the public accounts; the participation or holdings report; a sovereign or pension fund's published holdings; each ministry's or department's own organisation chart; and each ministry's own list of subordinate bodies, nachgeordnete Dienststellen, agencies or arm's-length bodies, which is usually published separately from the organisation chart and is where most of them sit.
Before the tree, state in 2 lines: whether a single official register of public bodies exists and its name, and whether a single official register of holdings exists and its name. Where either does not exist, say so plainly. That absence is the finding, and what follows is an assembly, not a register.
# Scope
This jurisdiction and the companies it owns.
Where a tier below it exists - state, province, region, county, district, municipality - do not expand it. Give the tier 1 row of its own: the tier's name, the number of bodies in it, and the status "tier not expanded". Each is its own Organization with its own register and its own Owners. Run this prompt again naming one of them.
# Output format
1 indented tree, box-drawing characters, or plain indentation if your tool breaks them.
1 line per entry:  name  · share held  · what it does  · [status]  (source number)
Omit share held for anything that is not a company. Omit [status] where the entry was expanded. Number the sources and list them once beneath the tree.
What it does: 7 words maximum. No adjectives, no assessment. Leave blank where you cannot say it in 7 words.
Status, where a branch stopped: "register ends here", "company, not expanded", "ownership not disclosed", "no owner found", "no current parent findable", "owned, statutorily independent", "independence not checked", "depth limit", "tier not expanded", "row limit", "not found".
Every node must be a body that exists and could receive a letter. Do not create grouping nodes, category headings or collective labels - no "agencies", no "state holdings", no "independent bodies". If you cannot write to it, it is not a node.
Order entries alphabetically within each level. Any other order is a category.
Where a group holds 30 members or fewer, name them all. Collapse to a count only above 30: "Bundeslaender (16)" must list the 16; "Gemeinden (10,750)" may not. Where you collapse, give the count in the row and never bury it: "Universities (23)" is a row a reader can see, "universities" is not. A collapsed row sits at the level its members sit at.
After the sources, add 1 line: "Ask for this as a table if you want to sort, filter or compare it against another run."
# Levels
The jurisdiction itself is the top level. Descending levels are negative, as in D32-R22-EXT: -1 is every body that sits under no other body, -2 the next level down, and so on.
# Depth
Level -1 holds more than 1 body. The head of state, the legislature, the courts, the audit institution and the central bank usually answer to no ministry. Do not force them under the head of government. State how many you found.
Public bodies: 5 levels. At level -5, mark the branch "depth limit" and state how many bodies sit below it if the register says.
Companies: expand exactly 1 level. Where a body holds a company, list it. Where that company publishes its own direct holdings, list them as 1 further level and stop. Mark the holding "company, not expanded".
Where a company publishes no holdings at all, mark it "ownership not disclosed". A chain that stops being published is not a disclosure.
Row limit: 2000. This is a safety valve, not a target. Long is better than partial: a tree that runs to 1000 rows is fine, and truncating a real structure to keep the output short is the worse failure.
Nothing here rewards length on its own. 50 sourced rows are worth more than 500 with 50 guessed among them. The limit came off so you can finish, not so you can fill.
Report how many pages you opened. Where you stop before the structure ends, say which branches you left open and under which parents.
# Where to go deep
Depth is not spread evenly. Go to level -5 on the branches an Owner actually meets: police and criminal investigation, tax and customs, courts and prosecution, prisons, benefits and employment, immigration and asylum, health and food safety, and the utilities they cannot leave. Stop at -3 on the rest.
An Owner needs to know which office acts on them, not how a ministry divides its internal paperwork.
# Placing a body correctly
Composition is parentage. Where a body is a constituent part of another - ministries of a government, chambers of a parliament, courts of a court system - place it beneath that body, not beside it. A level -1 that contains both a government and its own ministries is wrong.
A body with no owner is a level -1 body. Where no register, statute or report names anyone who holds or owns it - a public-law foundation, a self-governing corporation funded by compulsory contributions, a statutory chamber - mark it "no owner found" and do not attach it to a ministry because a ministry supervises it, funds it, or appoints part of its board. That is not ownership, and inventing a parent is as bad as inventing a body.
Ownership is not authority. Where a body is held by another but a statute, treaty or constitution bars the holder from instructing it, keep it under its holder and mark it "owned, statutorily independent", naming the instrument. Do not promote it: being owned is a fact about it, and deleting that fact to show independence loses more than it fixes. This applies most often to central banks, financial and competition regulators, data protection and election authorities, statistics offices and audit bodies. Where you cannot establish whether such a body is independent, mark it "independence not checked" rather than leaving it bare.
A central bank sits wherever its own country's law puts it: wholly state-held under a finance ministry, a shareless public institution at level -1, or a listed company with mixed owners. Take the position from the source, never from the pattern in another country.
Where a body's parent is named in a register but that parent no longer exists, place the body at level -1, mark it "no current parent findable", and name the defunct parent the register gives. Do not guess a successor.
Where a body has 2 parents by law - a joint authority of 2 levels of government, a body held by 2 ministries - place it under the first and add the second as a line marked [second parent, by law], naming the instrument. Do not pick one and drop the other.
Where a thread exists that is not ownership - appointment rights, legal supervision, a statutory basis, a shared service office - record it as a line marked [cross-link, not a parent], naming what the thread actually is.
# Natural persons
Name no people. No ministers, no board members, no directors, no office-holders, no shareholders who are natural persons. Where a natural person owns a company in a chain, write "held by a natural person, not named here" and stop that branch. This prompt maps structures.
# Rules
Do not state any body, company or holding from memory - your training data is likely outdated, and bodies are renamed, merged and abolished and holdings change constantly. Look each one up. Name the source for each row, not only for the tree.
Do not stop at ministries. A ministry with nothing under it is almost always a gap in the register, not a ministry with nothing under it. Open each one's own organisation chart and its own list of subordinate bodies.
Give every name as the register or the body publishes it. Add an English translation only where one is published. Never invent an English equivalent.
Where the register keys bodies to a ministry, check that those ministry names are the ones in force today. A register of what the state owns that cannot be joined to the list of who owns it is a finding; say so.
Mark "not published" where an item does not exist, with the reason; "not found" where it may exist and you did not find it - never report a failure to find as a failure to publish.
Use only numbers (good: "4 levels", wrong: "four levels").
No budgets, no revenues, no valuations, no staff counts, no founding dates, no transaction histories. This prompt produces a tree of names and nothing else.
# Finding sources
Never type a web address you have not seen. Every page you open must come from a search result, from a link on a page you already opened, or from me. Do not build a path on a domain you happen to know. A guessed address that resolves may hand you the wrong page or an old one and you will cite it as if it were right; a guessed address that fails costs a call and tells you nothing.
Search in iterations. Where the first search does not surface what you need, search again with different words before concluding anything: the body's exact legal name, the title of the document, the local-language term, the statute that created it, the audit institution's report on it, the parliament's documents. Use at least 2 differently worded searches before you mark anything "not found".
An error, a 404, a redirect or a refusal is never evidence about publication. A document you could not reach is "not reached"; a document that does not exist is "not published". Write the 2 apart and never let 1 stand in for the other.
A blocked source is a finding, not a stopping point. Where a site refuses automated access, is paywalled or needs a login, record it by name as "source exists, not reachable, a person can open this in a browser", give its address, and then try the alternatives above. Never let 1 refusal end a branch. Do not write that a document is unavailable when what happened is that it was unavailable to you.
# Minimum attempts
Before you output, you must have attempted at least 1 source for each of: head of state · legislature · constitutional or supreme court · audit institution · central bank · government and each of its ministries · each ministry's list of subordinate bodies · the holdings register · the tier below.
Report 2 numbers: attempted, and reached. A body you did not attempt is not the same as a body you could not find, and the output must not blur them. Name every body you dropped for want of a source.
# Density check
A ministry typically carries far more bodies than its organisation chart names - often 10 or more. Count what you found beneath each ministry and publish the count per ministry, lowest first. Where a ministry shows fewer than 5, mark it "likely under-reported" and name the sources you tried. Do not present a thin branch as a complete one. A count list that starts with several zeros is telling the reader what the run did not reach, which is the point.
# Completeness check
Run this only after the tree is built.
Print all 77 verdicts as a list, never as counts alone. A count hides a wrong verdict; a list shows it. Totals go beneath the list, not in place of it.
Before you print them, walk the tree once more: every body in the tree must appear against at least 1 function. A body you found and did not credit is a verdict error, and it is the commonest one.
Give each function 1 of 4 verdicts, never a blank:
  found         - name the body
  not found     - no body found in any register reached
  another tier  - performed by a level of government above or below this jurisdiction. Name the tier. A national map that omits health or schooling without saying they sit with the states is misleading, not incomplete.
  distributed   - no single body; several hold parts. Name them.
A body may serve more than 1 function; name it once and say so.
Where a function is performed by a body with no shareholder - a public-law foundation, a self-governing corporation funded by compulsory contributions, a statutory chamber - it is absent from shareholding registers by construction. Record it anyway and state that no register lists it.
Where a function is performed inside a ministry rather than by a separate body, say which ministry and which unit.

# Closing summary

# Word limit
Outside the tree, you have 200 words in total. That covers the 2 register lines, the completeness check wording and the closing summary together. Spend them where they say most.
Not counted and never shortened: the 2 notices, the 77 verdict lines, the counts, the sources, the status markers, the entry lines themselves and the question block. Write no other prose.
# Do not invent
An empty tree is a valid answer. A short tree is always better than a guessed one, and nothing here rewards a long tree.
Every entry must come from a page you opened in this run. Do not cite a source you did not open - if you cannot open it, the row does not go in.
Do not infer a body from a country's general pattern, from a neighbouring country, from a name that "should" exist, or from a structure you recall. Do not infer a subsidiary from a group name, a shared brand or a shared address. Do not carry a holding forward from an older report without saying which year it is from.
Before you output, check every row against the page it came from. Any row that fails comes out.
If you are uncertain whether an entry exists under that name today, it does not. Leave it out and say how many you dropped.
